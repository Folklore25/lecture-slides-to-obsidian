# Lecturer-context novelty policy

The goal is not to summarize the whole recording. Add only information that improves the existing slide-derived note.

## Keep

- explanation of why or how a slide statement works;
- concrete example, analogy, demonstration, or case;
- explicit lecturer emphasis or likely assessment cue;
- correction or qualification of slide wording;
- boundary condition, exception, failure mode, or practical caveat;
- answer to a student question;
- actionable logistics not already recorded.

## Exclude

- verbatim or near-verbatim repetition of existing note content;
- greetings, filler, false starts, microphone noise, attendance chatter;
- unsupported ASR fragments or uncertain names/numbers;
- generic transitions such as “next slide”;
- personal student information;
- an Agent inference not expressed by the lecturer.

## Comparison method

For each candidate:

1. identify the closest exact target H2/H3;
2. compare against that section and existing student/teacher callouts;
3. state one `novelty_basis` explaining what is new;
4. record an ASR timestamp/heading for the inserted callout and a short evidence fragment for the staging plan;
5. paraphrase the addition at the target note's altitude;
6. mark confidence `high`, `medium`, or `low`.

Only high/medium candidates may be applied. Low-confidence candidates remain review items.
